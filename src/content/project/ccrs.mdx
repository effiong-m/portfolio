highlights:
    - Designed cloning experiments and primers in SnapGene for BAFF-R based CCR variants, including mutations intended to activate and abrogate receptor signalling.
    - Executed the cloning process from DNA fragment generation, gel electrophoresis and extraction through seamless assembly, bacterial transformation, and maxipreps.
    - Used sequencing to validate construct identity, evaluate mutations, and confirm successfully assembled transfer plasmids.

results:
  - "Successfully cloned and validated lentiviral transfer plasmids containing BAFF-R based receptor constructs for downstream T-cell engineering."

  - "Generated BAFF-R CCR variants with targeted signalling modifications, including the H159Y activating mutation and a PVPAT to AVAAA signalling abrogating mutation."

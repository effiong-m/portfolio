highlights:
  - Used XTT assays to evaluate the effect of e-cigarette liquid concentration on A549 cell viability and identify a suitable treatment concentration.
  - Assessed cell viability across multiple treatment durations at a fixed 2% (v/v) e-liquid concentration to determine conditions for longer-term exposure.
  - Performed RNA isolation and cDNA synthesis to prepare cellular samples for downstream molecular analysis.
  - Measured IL25 and IL33 mRNA expression using qPCR to evaluate changes in type-2 inflammatory signalling over time.
  - Created Biorender diagrams to visually explain each experimental technique and incorporated them into the final course presentation.  

results:
  - Discovered findings that partially supported the hypothesis that with prolonged e-liquid exposure IL-25 and IL-33 expression would be upregulated in lung epithelial cells
  - A strong late increase in IL25 expression was observed while the IL33 response remained inconclusive.
